01  About

வணக்கம். I'm Ann, a first-year B.Tech IT student from Valliyur.

Most days I'm at Mariya Cars Valliyur, the used car dealership I run. We buy, sell, exchange, arrange finance and renew insurance on the spot. It taught me that people trust you when things are clear and quick, and I try to build software the same way.

I study IT so I can make the tools myself. So far that's two Flutter apps, a bilingual website for the dealership, and many evenings working out why something won't run on a real phone. I write too, in English and Tamil. Code and poems are closer than they look: both say something with as little as possible.
